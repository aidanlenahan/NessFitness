# NessFitness
## Inspiration
I had this idea once I started to go to the gym a lot more. Ever since I first started going to the gym back during the summer before my freshman year of high school, I'd write down my workouts in the Notes app of my iPhone. What I realized after years of doing that was that I couldn't easily export these files. I wanted to visualize my progress, be able to search through my hundreds of workouts, and even maybe optimize the visualizations with AI. But I couldn't do that.


Recently I started using [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/task-management) as my go-to to-do list. It has this really cool feature where you can use certain special characters to signify different things while writing a to-do list item. For example, wrapping a date in `{}` will automatically parse as the due date for that task. putting a `!` in front of a date and time will automatically parse as a time you'd like to be notified. Using the `#` could specify *which* list you wanted this task in. There are [a bunch more operators for this](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-task-quick-add-in-todoist-va4Lhpzz), all of which save time and make it frictionless to make a task. This is how I wanted the fitness tracker to be. In the gym, you want to get an efficient workout done. So the goal of this was to be as frictionless as Todoist, with operators able to parse how many reps, sets, and weight a particular exercise was done. One could even add operators such as `+` and `-` to signify whether that weight was light for them and they could go up in weight, or vice versa. After a few workouts had been tracked, digestible data could be visualized to see one's progress.
## Why I stopped
Life. Life got in the way. I was very busy at the time of starting this. Not to mention, I did not have expertise in the languages and architecture this app would have relied on, which added to the difficulty and made it less desirable to want to do this project. This could be something I resume down the line, but for now, it goes into the archive.
